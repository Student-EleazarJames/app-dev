## Puss in Boots: The Last Wish
When it comes to films, the very first installment is usually never toppled by its sequels, but Puss in Boots is not one of them. And it's mostly
due to the fact that the first movie was not all too great. However, it was not a bad start to the Puss in Boots series of films. After the first
film, companies would usually just leave it just like that, having no sequels at all. Not many people expected for this spin off film to have one
and yet, it still did.

What made Puss in Boots: The Last Wish better than its predecessor is not just because the first was not all too great, but it's due to the fact
that they went way beyond for what we expect for this series from its very first landing.
- Beautiful Animation
- Well Handled Expressions and Characterization
- Amazing Story Writing
- and many more
Despite being a sequel, this movie can be watched without the knowledge of the first film, making it very beginner-friendly to watch. An absolute
cinematic *masterpiece* that you don't want to miss. One of my very favorite scenes of the film was definitely the final showdown between Puss in
Boots and a character I do not want to spoil, you have to watch the film to know what I'm talking about, it was one of the film's most defining
moments. Thank you for reading through all of this, and hope you get the opportunity to watch this amazing film, but if you already did, was it
a fun watch for you? Other than that, have a great day!
